Installing

  1. Download the APK above and allow installs from your browser when prompted.
  2. Open the app once and grant location access, then set it to “Allow all the time” in your device's app settings so the widgets can refresh in the background.
  3. On Samsung devices: go to Settings → Apps → Solar Sentinel → Battery → Unrestricted, so background refresh isn't paused by battery optimization.
